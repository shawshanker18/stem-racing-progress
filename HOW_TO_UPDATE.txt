How a part's builder updates this board

1. Edit only your own file in the progress folder (part1 to part5). Never touch another part's file. Pull right before you edit, and push straight after, so you don't clash with the redraw.

2. TIMELINE ("tasks"): your build steps, in order. Keep it current; this is how the team lead follows you without asking.
   - When you START a step: set its "status" to "doing" and "started" to today's date (YYYY-MM-DD).
   - When you FINISH a step and its tests pass on GitHub: "status" "done", "finished" = today's date.
   - If a step's tests fail and you're redoing it: "status" "failed" (set it back to "doing" when you restart).
   - If you're waiting on another part or on the team lead: "status" "blocked", and say why in "note".
   - "note": one short plain sentence about where the step stands.
   - Allowed statuses: todo, doing, done, failed, blocked. Keep "title" and "short" as they are (short is at most 14 characters; it's what the picture shows).
   - Only one or two steps should be "doing" at a time.

3. SQUARES ("sections" / "items"): raise "done" as work and tests pass. Set "failed" for tests that failed and are being redone, and lower it again when they pass. Keep each item's "short" label (12 characters at most).

3b. UNLOCKS (Parts 1, 2 and 4 only): when you send the team lead an UNLOCK message, ALSO set that milestone in your file's "unlocks" to "done": true, with "date" (YYYY-MM-DD) and a one-sentence "note". The live panel's "Ready to start?" box reads this to tell the team lead when Part 3 and Part 5 can start. Part 1 owns A; Part 2 owns B and C; Part 4 owns D. Only mark it done when its tests passed on GitHub.

3c. YOUR STATE (the live panel shows it as "Agent N"): set "agent_state" to one of
    working             you are working (reading, coding, preparing) and nothing of yours runs on GitHub
    waiting_on_compute  your runs are going on GitHub and you are waiting for results
    needs_you           you asked the team lead something; put the question in "needs_you" (one plain sentence)
    blocked             you are waiting on another part; say which in "agent_note"
  and "agent_note" to one plain sentence. Change it the moment your state changes (for example right after you
  start a run, and again when its results are in), and clear "needs_you" once answered. If you don't update for
  3 hours the panel shows you as QUIET.

4. Also update "now" (one plain sentence), "updated" (the date), "status" (waiting, active or done), and add one line to "log" for anything worth remembering.

5. Plain words only. No code, file names, links, settings, measurements of the car, or anything that would let someone rebuild the work. The redraw refuses text that looks like code, a path or a link.

6. Commit and push. The board, the timelines and the pages redraw themselves in about a minute.
